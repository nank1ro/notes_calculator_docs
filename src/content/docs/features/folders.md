Folders group your notes, like folders in a file browser. A note is in one
folder or in none. Deleting a folder never deletes its notes.

## Creating a folder

Click the **New folder** button, next to the **New note** button at the top
of the notes sidebar (hover a button to see its name), or press
**Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + N**. Name the folder and it appears in the list. In the
**Picker** layout, **New folder** is also at the bottom of the folder menu.

The shortcut works in the desktop and tablet apps, not in the web app.

Folders show in the **Sidebar** notes layout, and on phones (see
[On phones](#on-phones)). With the **Tabs** layout they are hidden: your
notes keep their folders, and a new note goes to no folder. Choose **Sidebar**
in **Settings**, **Appearance**, **Notes layout** to see them again.

## The sidebar

Click a folder to open it in place and see its notes under it. Click it again
to close it. Each folder shows how many notes it has. Notes in no folder are
listed after your folders, under **Notes**.

Folders look different in each of three layouts. Choose one in **Settings**,
**Appearance**, **Folders** (computers, the web and tablets, with the
**Sidebar** notes layout):

- **Tree** (the default): folders open in place, as above. Search looks through
  every folder and shows only the folders with matching notes.
- **Sections**: **All notes**, then your folders, sit above your notes. Click
  one to see only its notes. Search looks in the folder you're looking at.
  Under **All notes**, each note in a folder shows its folder.
- **Picker**: a folder menu at the top of the sidebar. Search looks in the
  folder you're looking at, and under **All notes** each note in a folder
  shows its folder.

A new note goes to the folder of the note you have open. In **Sections** and
**Picker**, it goes to the folder you're looking at.

## Moving notes

- Drag a note onto a folder, with a mouse or trackpad (computers and the web
  on a computer). In the **Picker** layout, use **Move to folder** instead.
- Or open the note's menu and choose **Move to folder**. In the sidebar,
  right-click the note, use its **⋯** button, or long-press it on a tablet
  (this is the way to move a note on a tablet). On a phone, use the **⋯** of
  the note in the notes list.

Choose **No folder** in **Move to folder** to take a note out of its folder.

## Reordering, renaming and deleting folders

Drag a folder to reorder it, with a mouse or trackpad. Right-click a folder,
or use its **⋯** button, to rename it, move it up, move it down or delete it.
On a tablet, long-press a folder for the same menu: **Move up** and
**Move down** reorder it.

Deleting a folder never deletes its notes: they're kept, outside any folder.

## On phones

Phones have no sidebar and no layout choice. Tap the title at the top of your
notes to pick a folder. In that list:

- each folder's **⋯** renames, moves or deletes it;
- **New folder**, at the top right, adds one.

A note's **⋯** menu in the notes list has **Move to folder**, with
**New folder** at the top right too. The round **New note** button (the pen
icon) adds the new note to the folder you're looking at.

## Turning folders off

Don't want folders? Turn them off with the switch in **Settings**,
**Appearance**, **Folders**. It is there on every device. Your notes then show
as one list. Your folders, and the notes in them, come back when you turn
folders on again.

## Sync

When you're signed in, folders sync across your devices (see
[Plans, Accounts and Sync](/features/plans_and_sync/#sync)). A device with an
older version of the app doesn't show folders, but keeps each note in its
folder.
