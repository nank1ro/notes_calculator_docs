Notes Calculator has two plans: the free plan and Lifetime.

- **Free plan:** one note. The sample note new users start with doesn't
  count: a new note replaces it until you edit it.
- **Lifetime:** a one-time purchase, with no subscription. It unlocks
  unlimited notes and syncs them across your devices. One purchase unlocks
  Lifetime on every platform.

On the free plan, a [shared note](/features/sharing/#opening-a-shared-note)
you open while you already have a note opens read-only, so your note stays
as it is.

## Your account

Your account is on the **Account** page:

- **Phones:** tap the account icon on the notes list.
- **Tablets:** tap the **Account** tab.
- **Desktop and web:** open the command palette (**Cmd/Ctrl + K**) and
  choose **Profile**.

Buying Lifetime needs an account, so you're asked to create a free one first.
The notes on your device move into your account, then checkout opens. On
iPhone and iPad you buy Lifetime through the App Store, and **Restore
Purchases** brings back a purchase you've already made.

## Sync

With Lifetime, your notes sync to every device you sign in to. Changes from
your other devices show up in an open note, and a note you delete on one
device is deleted on the others.

## Signing out and deleting your account

Signing out of a free account keeps your note on the device: the note you
wrote before signing in stays in the app, with your edits. Deleting a free
account keeps the note on the device too.

**Delete account** on the Account page permanently deletes your account and
your synced notes, and signs you out. It can't be undone.
