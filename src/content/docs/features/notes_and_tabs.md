On computers, the web and tablets, your notes are listed in a sidebar on the
left, and you click a note to open it. If you prefer tabs, each note opens in
its own tab instead (see [Tabs](#tabs)). On phones, the app opens on your
notes list and each note opens as its own page (see [On phones](#on-phones)).
New users start with a short sample note that shows what Notes Calculator can
do.

A note is named after its heading: start a new note with `# Trip budget` and
it is called Trip budget. If you delete the heading, the note is called
Untitled until you type another one. To rename a note yourself, double-click
it in the sidebar (or select it and press **F2**), or tap its title on a
phone.

To group your notes, see [Folders](/features/folders/).

## The notes sidebar

### Search and sort

Type in the search box at the top of the sidebar to filter your notes. Press
**Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + F** to jump to it. Search ignores accents and finds notes
even when letters are missing in between: `cae` finds "Café". The note search
(**Cmd/Ctrl + P**) works the same way.

Sort the list by **Last updated**, **Date created**, or name (**Name A to Z**
or **Name Z to A**).

### The note menu

Right-click a note in the sidebar, or use its **⋯** button, to **Rename**,
**Share link**, **Export** or **Delete** it, or to **Move to folder** (see
[Folders](/features/folders/)). On iOS and Android tablets, long-press the
note. For sharing and exporting, see
[Sharing a Note as a Link](/features/sharing/).

You're asked to confirm before a note is deleted, and the question names the
note. After you delete the note you're on, the app goes back to the note you
were viewing before it.

### Keyboard and mouse

- Hold **Cmd** (**Ctrl** on Windows and Linux) to see the numbers of the first
  nine notes in the sidebar, then press **Cmd/Ctrl + 1** to **Cmd/Ctrl + 9**
  to open one.
- **Cmd/Ctrl + S** hides or shows the sidebar.
- Drag the edge of the sidebar to make it wider or narrower.
- Each device remembers the sidebar's width, its sort order and whether it's
  hidden.

### Next and previous note

Swipe sideways over a note, or press **Cmd/Ctrl + ]** for the next note and
**Cmd/Ctrl + [** for the previous one. They follow the order of the sidebar,
in the folder and search you're looking at.

**Ctrl + Tab** and **Ctrl + Shift + Tab**, or **Ctrl + Page Down** and
**Ctrl + Page Up**, switch notes too. With the sidebar, **Page Down** and
**Page Up** do the same.

## Tabs

To use tabs instead of the sidebar, open **Settings**, **Appearance**,
**Notes layout** and choose **Tabs**. Choose **Sidebar** to go back. Phones
have no choice: they always show the notes list.

With tabs, each note opens in its own tab. Double-click a tab to rename the
note.

The tab menu has the actions for one note: closing tabs, **Export** and
**Share link** (see [Sharing a Note as a Link](/features/sharing/)), and
**Delete**. After you close or delete the tab you're on, the app goes back to
the note you were viewing before it.

- **Desktop and web:** right-click a tab.
- **iOS and Android tablets:** long-press a tab.

## On phones

A phone is an iPhone or Android phone, or a phone's browser using the web
app. Tablets and computers have the sidebar or tabs.

The notes list is the home screen. Pull it down to get the notes you changed
on your other devices (see [Plans, Accounts and Sync](/features/plans_and_sync/#sync)).
Tap a note to open it as its own page, with **Back** to return to the list,
the note's title, and a **⋯** menu with **New note**, **Rename**,
**Find in note**, **Share link**, **Export**, **Settings**,
**Documentation** and **Delete**. If you left a note open, it
opens again when you come back to the app.

Each note in the list has a **⋯** menu too, with **Share link**, **Export**,
**Move to folder** and **Delete**. Long-press a note to open the same menu,
or swipe it to the left to delete it. Deleting a note asks you to confirm,
and renaming opens a text field: both in a sheet that slides up from the
bottom, where **Back** takes you to the previous step.

## Note lists

All your notes are listed in the sidebar on computers, the web and tablets,
in **Search Buffer** (**Cmd/Ctrl + P**, or the search icon), in the **Notes**
tab on iOS and Android tablets, and on the notes list on phones.

By default the most recently updated notes are on top. Under each title you
see when the note was last updated and when it was created, for example
"Updated 5 minutes ago | Created Oct 1, 2026". When both read the same, as
for a note that was never edited, only "Updated" is shown.

Dates from the last week read like "2 days ago"; older ones show the date
itself. With **Use European date format** on, dates read day first, like
"1 Oct 2026".
