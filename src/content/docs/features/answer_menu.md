Every line has an answer menu.

- **Desktop:** hover over the answer and click the small **⋯** button next to it.
- **Mobile:** long-press the answer, or the empty space where it would be.

## Copy answer

Copies the answer exactly as it's shown.

## Decimal places

Drag the slider to show between 0 and 10 decimal places for this answer only.
**Reset** goes back to your decimals setting.

```
10.12345                                       | 10.1235
```

Rounding here only changes what you see. `total`, `prev` and line references
keep using the full value.

## Custom text

Shows your own text instead of the answer. It works on any line, including
headings and empty lines, which makes it handy for column labels:

```
# Spins                                        | Total
10                                             | 10
20                                             | 20
total                                          | 30
```

Custom text is display only: calculations below keep using the real value.
To remove it, open the menu again, choose **Edit custom text**, and click
**Clear** (the button only appears once a line has custom text).

Custom text and decimal places are saved with the note, sync to your other
devices, and appear in every export format.
